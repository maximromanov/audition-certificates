Damascus Certificates × Taʾrīkh al-Islām

25 audition certificates recording sessions held in Damascus between 550 and 700 AH, drawn from the Audition Certificates Platform dataset. Participants are linked to biographies in al-Dhahabī's Taʾrīkh al-Islām. Click a highlighted name to read the matched biography and confirm or reject the match.

Background image source: MS Staatsbibliothek Berlin, Wetzstein II 150, 1v
Certificates: Audition Certificates Platform (CSMC, Universität Hamburg), data release msnotesannotator_audition_certificates_data. Biographies: al-Dhahabī, Taʾrīkh al-Islām, OpenITI text 0748Dhahabi.TarikhIslam.MGR20180917-ara1. This is an independent research prototype whose layout follows the ACP; it is not affiliated with the platform. Matches were proposed by an onomastic matcher and adjudicated by a reviewer (Claude), and they await scholarly review.
